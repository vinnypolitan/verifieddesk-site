Questions operators ask

Straight answers.

How is this different from a directory I can find myself?

Public directories list organizations. A referral source map tells you which ones actually place residents, who inside the organization handles placements, and whether the program is still operating, with a source link on every row so you can check the work. The hours of calling dead numbers is the part we remove.

Do you cover my county?

Any United States county. The map is built fresh per county from state and county sources, not pulled from a national database, which is why the contacts hold up.

What does the free sample include?

Ten referral organizations in your county, with the same columns as the full map: organization, type, named placement contact where published, who they serve, and a source link per row. No charge, no call, keep it either way.

What does a full county map cost?

Fixed price, quoted after the free sample so the quote reflects your county's actual size. No hourly billing. Rows that turn out wrong are replaced free.

Is my information shared with anyone?

No. Maps are built per client and not resold, and we do not share who our clients are.
