Questions agencies ask

Straight answers.

Does our client ever find out VerifiedDesk exists?

No. Deliverables carry your brand or no brand, files are metadata-clean, and we never contact your client. Non-circumvention goes in writing on the first project: your clients are yours, permanently.

What can you deliver behind us?

Automation and AI workflow builds (Python, n8n, Make, Zapier, GoHighLevel), verified B2B lead lists with per-row source links and mail-server checks, and cited research reports. If a spec is outside that, we say so at quote time instead of learning on your client.

How does pricing work?

Fixed price per project, quoted same day from your spec, confirmed in writing before work starts. You set your own client pricing on top; the margin is the point. No hourly billing, no retainers required.

What happens if the deliverable has defects?

Every delivery ships with the QA report that proves it was checked. Provable defects against the written spec are fixed free. Bad rows in data deliverables are replaced free.

Can you handle overflow only, or do you need committed volume?

Overflow is fine. Send one project when your bench is full and nothing when it is not. Committed monthly volume earns priority scheduling and better rates, but nothing is required.
